# FEE3411 — Control Systems A

Department of Electrical and Information Engineering, University of Nairobi.

Everything here is written for this unit: full lecture notes you can read on a
phone, tutorial sheets with worked solutions, and the assignments. The notes are
**self-contained** — you can learn the material from them without the textbook.
The set texts are there to reinforce and to give you more problems, not as a
prerequisite.

## What you should be able to do by the end

| | Outcome | Weeks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Describe the working principles of a given control system | 1, 2 |
| 2 | Analyse a control system solution in the time- and frequency-domains | 2–6, 9, 10, 11 |
| 3 | Design a control system to control operations of a simple application | 9 (introductory; developed in FEE3412) |
| 4 | Analyse the stability of a control system and design a strategy for its enhancement | 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12 |

FEE3411 is the **analysis** half. Design is introduced in Week 9 and developed
properly in FEE3412.
